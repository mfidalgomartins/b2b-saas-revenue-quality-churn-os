# Goal — bring the analytical report to a 10/10 content bar

_Authored 2026-06-20 from the current state of `scripts/build_pdf_report.py` and
`outputs/reports/revenue_quality_os_analytical_report.pdf`._

## Current state

The report already has the right production surface: a designed PDF generated by
ReportLab, a coherent executive structure, numbered exhibits, tables, caveats,
recommendations, and a reproducible data trail. The design should remain unchanged.

The remaining gap is content depth, not visual presentation. To read like a
Big-4-quality report, the analysis needs more explicit decision logic, stronger
bridges between metrics and management actions, clearer sensitivity framing, and
more audit-grade interpretation around the high-risk cohort, discount discipline,
expansion quality, score calibration, and forecast cases.

## Constraints

- Preserve the existing design, palette, fonts, layout helpers, chart pack, and PDF
  generation approach.
- Expand only the analysis, rigor, completeness, and executive interpretation.
- Keep the writing natural, specific, and evidence-led.
- Avoid generic consulting filler and AI-sounding prose.
- Keep all claims tied to the existing processed data, report metrics, validation
  summary, backtest output, sensitivity report, and scenario tables.

## Content upgrades required

1. **Executive depth.** Strengthen the summary so it explains not just what is
   happening, but why it matters for board-level decisions.
2. **Method and governance confidence.** Make validation, data lineage, and release
   controls visible enough for trust without turning the report into a technical
   appendix.
3. **Finding-level rigor.** For each major finding, add the missing so-what:
   management implication, decision rule, operational threshold, or follow-up test.
4. **Risk-cohort precision.** Align account counts and exposure language with the
   current scoring output: 79 High and 1 Critical governance-priority accounts,
   representing 80 High/Critical accounts and $376k of current MRR.
5. **Forecast usefulness.** Connect scenarios to budget decisions, intervention
   economics, and monthly review triggers.
6. **Recommendation completeness.** Add operating cadence, ownership logic, success
   metrics, and sequencing so the recommendations are directly executable.
7. **Caveat maturity.** Keep the associative-not-causal discipline, but explain
   exactly which follow-up tests would raise confidence.

## Definition of done

The generated PDF keeps the same visual design, but the narrative is more rigorous,
more complete, and more decision-ready. It should stand on its own as a polished
advisory report: answer-first, evidence-backed, commercially specific, candid about
limitations, and directly actionable.
